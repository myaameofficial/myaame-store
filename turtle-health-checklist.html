Free Turtle Health Checklist

Download this free printable Turtle Health Checklist to monitor your Red-Eared Slider's health every day. Catch early warning signs before they become serious problems.

📖 6 min read • Last Updated: July 2026

Quick Answer

Healthy turtles usually eat well, swim normally, bask every day and have bright open eyes. This printable checklist helps you perform a quick daily health check in less than one minute so you can recognise early signs of illness.

Why Use This Checklist?

  • Monitor your turtle's health in under one minute.
  • Spot early warning signs before they become emergencies.
  • Keep track of aquarium conditions.
  • Perfect for beginner turtle parents.
  • Printable and completely free.

What's Inside?

The My.Aame Turtle Health Checklist was created to help turtle parents quickly identify common signs of good health and recognise when something may be wrong.

Instead of wondering whether your turtle looks normal, you can compare its behaviour against a simple daily checklist.

Daily Health Checklist

  • ✔ Eating normally
  • ✔ Swimming normally
  • ✔ Basking every day
  • ✔ Eyes open and clear
  • ✔ Shell firm (not soft)
  • ✔ Nose free of bubbles
  • ✔ Breathing normally
  • ✔ Skin free from wounds or swelling
  • ✔ Passing normal poop
  • ✔ Active and alert

Aquarium Checklist

  • ✔ Water temperature correct
  • ✔ UVB lamp working
  • ✔ Heat lamp working
  • ✔ Filter running properly
  • ✔ Water looks clean

Seek Help Immediately If You Notice

  • ❌ No food for more than 7 days
  • ❌ Eyes won't open
  • ❌ Heavy wheezing
  • ❌ Open-mouth breathing
  • ❌ Floating sideways
  • ❌ Soft shell
  • ❌ Blood or major injuries

Small changes are often the first signs of illness. Recognising these changes early can significantly improve your turtle's chances of recovery.
