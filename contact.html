Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the consultation?

Most consultations take around 30–45 minutes, depending on your turtle's situation.

Is this a veterinary consultation?

No. I am not a veterinarian. I provide educational guidance based on practical turtle care experience. If I believe your turtle needs medical treatment, I'll recommend that you consult a qualified reptile veterinarian.

Can I send photos and videos?

Yes. Clear photos and videos of your turtle and habitat help me provide more accurate guidance.

Which turtle species do you help with?

I primarily help Red-Eared Sliders and other commonly kept freshwater turtles. If you're unsure, feel free to ask before booking.

Important: My consultations are educational and husbandry-focused. I am not a licensed veterinarian. If your turtle requires medical diagnosis, surgery or prescription medication, I will always recommend consulting a qualified reptile veterinarian.
