✍️ Why I Wrote This Guide

I Wrote the Feeding Guide I Could Never Find.

One of the questions I receive more than any other is simple: "What can I feed my turtle?" The problem is that the internet is full of conflicting food lists. One website says spinach is good. Another says never feed it. One person recommends fruit every day, another says never.

I wanted one place where turtle parents could quickly check whether a food was safe, understand why it was safe (or unsafe), and know how often it should be fed.

That's why I created The Red-Eared Slider Food Bible. It's a practical feeding handbook covering more than 60 foods, balanced nutrition, feeding schedules and common mistakes—so you can spend less time searching online and more time caring for your turtle.

— Ramkumar K
