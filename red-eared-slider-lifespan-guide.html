How Long Do Red-Eared Sliders Live?

Discover how long Red-Eared Sliders live, what affects their lifespan, and how proper care can help your turtle live a healthy life for decades.

📖 6 min read • Last Updated: July 2026

Quick Answer

A healthy Red-Eared Slider (RES) can live 20–40 years in captivity, while wild turtles usually live 20–30 years. Proper nutrition, UVB lighting, filtration, clean water and regular health care play a major role in helping them reach their full lifespan.
