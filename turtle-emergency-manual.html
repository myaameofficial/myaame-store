I Wrote The Book I Wish Every Turtle Parent Had.

Over the years, I've spoken with hundreds of turtle parents. Most of them genuinely wanted to do the right thing—but they were overwhelmed by conflicting advice, outdated information and internet myths.

I realised that turtle parents didn't need more information. They needed one practical guide they could trust. So I created The Turtle Parent's Emergency Manual—a step-by-step resource designed to help you make better decisions with confidence and give your turtle the healthy life it deserves.
