🐢 ABOUT MY.AAME

Helping You Raise a Healthier, Happier Turtle.

I'm Ram, the founder of My.Aame. After seeing how much conflicting turtle care advice exists online, I decided to create a place where turtle parents could find practical, honest, and easy-to-understand guidance based on real experience and research. My goal is simple: help you make better decisions so your turtle can live a healthier life.

My Story

It Started With Two Turtles.

Like many first-time turtle parents, I believed I was doing everything right. But the more I learned, the more I realized how much outdated and conflicting information existed online.

Instead of accepting the confusion, I began researching, learning from experienced keepers, reading scientific resources, and improving my own turtle care every day.

That journey eventually became My.Aame—a place where turtle parents can find practical, honest, and easy-to-understand guidance without the myths and misinformation.

What I Believe

Every Turtle Deserves Better Information.

Most turtle owners genuinely want to do the right thing. The problem isn't a lack of care—it's a lack of reliable information.

My.Aame exists to simplify turtle care through practical education, honest recommendations, and real-world experience so every turtle parent can make confident decisions.

Meet Shadow & Light

The Two Turtles Behind My.Aame

Shadow and Light have been at the heart of my journey. Caring for them taught me that good turtle keeping isn't about expensive products or complicated routines—it's about understanding their needs and making informed decisions every day.

Everything I create through My.Aame is inspired by the lessons they've taught me and the goal of helping other turtle parents give their turtles the best possible life.

Ready to Become a Better Turtle Parent?

Whether you're just starting out or looking to improve your turtle's care, My.Aame is here to help with practical guides, trusted resources, and easy-to-follow education.
